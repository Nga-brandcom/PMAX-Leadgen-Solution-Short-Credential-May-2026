01 · The Shift

The next growth chapter won't be won by who collects more Leads.

For the last decade, Vietnamese enterprises have measured leadgen success in Raw Lead volume. That model has run out of leverage. In 2026, the winners are the businesses — and the partners — that take accountability for the lower funnel, where revenue is actually decided.

PMAX Leadgen Solution exists to be that partner.

01 · The top-of-funnel trap

A lot of Leads in. Very few customers out.

CMO reports "Lead +200%." CFO asks one question: "Where is the new revenue?" Traditional agencies stop at Lead handover — with no accountability for the conversion stage where business growth is actually decided.

02 · Loss of data ownership

The business doesn't own its own audience.

With 3–5 partners each holding a fragment of the data, conversion signals and customer profiles disappear when campaigns end. No first-party data asset. No long-term competitive moat.

03 · Hitting the growth ceiling

Old playbooks have run out of runway.

In-house teams cannot operate at 3–5× scale. Optimization technology cannot keep up with non-linear volume. No cross-industry benchmark to know where the real ceiling sits.

04 · A fragmented operating system

It's not a people problem — it's a system gap.

Agencies stop at the Lead. Sales has no source visibility. Marketing has no feedback loop. Every team on its own KPI, with no single source of truth across the funnel.
